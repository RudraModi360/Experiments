# Syllabus: Grade 10 Mathematics
## Unit 1: Algebra (Weeks 1-3)
- Linear equations and inequalities
- Systems of equations (substitution, elimination)
- Polynomials and factoring
## Unit 2: Geometry (Weeks 4-6)
- Triangles (congruence, similarity)
- Circles (tangents, chords, arcs)
- Coordinate geometry (distance, midpoint, slope)
## Unit 3: Statistics & Probability (Weeks 7-8)
- Mean, median, mode, standard deviation
- Probability basics, combinations
## Exam Prep (Week 9)
- Review all units, practice tests
