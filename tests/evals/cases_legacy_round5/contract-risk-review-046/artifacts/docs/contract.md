# Service Agreement

1. Term: 12 months, auto-renews unless 90 days notice.
2. Termination: Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days notice; vendor may terminate immediately for non-payment.
3. Liability: Vendor liability capped at fees paid in last 3 months; excludes indirect damages.
4. Indemnity: Client indemnifies vendor for third-party claims.
5. Data: Vendor may use aggregated data for analytics.
