# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This document compiles frequently asked questions based on the official documentation corpus, grouped by topic with source citations.

## Getting Started

### Q: How do I create an account?
* **Answer:** Go to the signup page, enter your email address, and verify your account.
* **Source:** [docs/getting-started.md](docs/getting-started.md)

### Q: What browsers are supported?
* **Answer:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest 2 versions).
* **Source:** [docs/getting-started.md](docs/getting-started.md)

### Q: Is there a mobile app?
* **Answer:** Yes, iOS and Android apps are available.
* **Source:** [docs/getting-started.md](docs/getting-started.md)

---

## API & Integration

### Q: Is there an API?
* **Answer:** Yes, a REST API with JSON support. Full documentation is available at `/api/docs`.
* **Source:** [docs/api.md](docs/api.md)

### Q: What are the rate limits?
* **Answer:** 1,000 requests per minute for Pro users, and 100 requests per minute for Free users.
* **Source:** [docs/api.md](docs/api.md)

### Q: How does authentication work?
* **Answer:** Authentication is done using a Bearer token passed in the `Authorization` header.
* **Source:** [docs/api.md](docs/api.md)

---

## Billing & Subscriptions

### Q: What payment methods are accepted?
* **Answer:** Credit card, PayPal, and bank transfer.
* **Source:** [docs/billing.md](docs/billing.md)

### Q: Can I get a refund?
* **Answer:** Yes, refunds are available within 30 days of purchase.
* **Source:** [docs/billing.md](docs/billing.md)

### Q: How do I upgrade my plan?
* **Answer:** Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Upgrade**.
* **Source:** [docs/billing.md](docs/billing.md)

### Q: Are there annual discounts?
* **Answer:** Yes, 20% off annual plans.
* **Source:** [docs/billing.md](docs/billing.md)

---

## Troubleshooting

### Q: App won't load?
* **Answer:** Clear your cache and cookies, or try opening in incognito mode.
* **Source:** [docs/troubleshooting.md](docs/troubleshooting.md)

### Q: Forgot password?
* **Answer:** Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page.
* **Source:** [docs/troubleshooting.md](docs/troubleshooting.md)

### Q: Data not syncing?
* **Answer:** Check your internet connection, then go to **Settings > Sync > Force sync**.
* **Source:** [docs/troubleshooting.md](docs/troubleshooting.md)
