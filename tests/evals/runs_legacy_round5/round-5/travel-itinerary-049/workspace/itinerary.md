# Itinerary: December 20 - December 24, 2026

- **Destination / Trip Dates:** December 20 – December 24, 2026 (5 days, 4 nights)
- **Total Budget:** $2,000
- **Interests:** History, Food, Walking

---

## Day 1: December 20, 2026 – Arrival & Historic Exploration
- **Morning / Afternoon:** Arrive at destination via flight ($600 RT). Check in to hotel ($120/night).
- **Late Afternoon:** Self-guided walking tour of the Old Town / Historic Quarter. Explore ancient cobblestone streets, architecture, and monuments.
- **Evening:** Welcome dinner at a traditional local restaurant tasting regional specialties.

## Day 2: December 21, 2026 – History & Culinary Heritage
- **Morning:** Visit the National History Museum ($30 admission) to explore regional history and ancient artifacts.
- **Afternoon:** Scenic walking tour along the historic riverfront and fortress walls ($25 guided walking tour).
- **Evening:** Evening food tour ($80) sampling local street food, markets, and artisanal beverages.

## Day 3: December 22, 2026 – Deep Dive into Local Culture
- **Morning:** Walking exploration of the historic district's artisan markets and heritage sites.
- **Afternoon:** Visit a secondary historical landmark / palace museum.
- **Evening:** Dinner at a highly-rated historic tavern.

## Day 4: December 23, 2026 – Scenic Walks & Gastronomy
- **Morning:** Scenic nature and historical walking trail in the surrounding hills/parks.
- **Afternoon:** Free time for cafe hopping and souvenir shopping in the old town.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner featuring local gastronomy and wine pairings.

## Day 5: December 24, 2026 – Departure
- **Morning:** Leisurely breakfast, final morning stroll, check out of hotel.
- **Afternoon:** Depart for airport / return flight home.
