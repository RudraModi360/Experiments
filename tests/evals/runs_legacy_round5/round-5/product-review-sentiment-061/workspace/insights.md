# Customer Review Insights & Sentiment Analysis

## Overview
This report provides a sentiment and thematic analysis of customer reviews (`reviews.csv`). A total of 5 reviews were analyzed across ratings ranging from 1 to 5.

## Sentiment Distribution
- **Positive (4-5 stars):** 40% (2 reviews) - Average Score: 1.0
- **Neutral (3 stars):** 20% (1 review) - Average Score: 0.0
- **Negative (1-2 stars):** 40% (2 reviews) - Average Score: -0.75

## Key Themes
1. **Product Quality & Durability:**
   - Positive sentiment highlights satisfaction with core product value ("Great product love it", "Excellent value").
   - Negative sentiment highlights durability issues ("Terrible broke after a week").
2. **Pricing & Value Proposition:**
   - Customers weigh value against cost; perception of poor pricing is a driver of negative sentiment ("Not worth the price").
3. **Logistics & Delivery:**
   - Shipping speed is a noted friction point even in neutral experiences ("Okay but shipping slow").

## Top Complaints
1. **Product Durability / Early Breakage:** Items failing shortly after delivery (1-star rating).
2. **Pricing / Value Mismatch:** Products perceived as overpriced relative to quality (2-star rating).
3. **Slow Shipping / Fulfillment Delays:** Delayed delivery times impacting overall satisfaction (3-star rating).

## Recommendations
- **Quality Control:** Investigate manufacturing standards to address early breakage issues.
- **Logistics Partner Review:** Evaluate shipping partners to improve delivery speed and reduce neutral/negative friction.
- **Value Communication:** Clearly communicate product features and value proposition to align customer price expectations.
