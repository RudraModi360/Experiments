# Clinical Trial Summary: Drug X vs Placebo

## Overview
This document summarizes the results of the clinical trial comparing **Drug X** against **Placebo**.

## Trial Arms
- **Drug X**: $n = 100$
- **Placebo**: $n = 100$

## Endpoints

| Endpoint Type | Endpoint Name | Drug X | Placebo | P-Value |
|--------------|--------------|--------|---------|---------|
| Primary | Recovery Rate | 78% | 55% | 0.001 |
| Secondary | Time to Recovery | 5.2 days | 8.1 days | 0.003 |

## Safety & Adverse Events
- **Drug X**: 12% adverse event rate
- **Placebo**: 8% adverse event rate

## Conclusion
Drug X demonstrated statistically significant improvements in both the primary recovery rate ($p = 0.001$) and secondary time to recovery ($p = 0.003$) compared to Placebo, with a modest increase in adverse events (12% vs 8%).
